# Visual review

Two reviewing agents received anonymously named strips of six sampled frames per clip. They scored the readable premise, downstream motion and spatial coherence, composition and stability, and visible technical execution. Each criterion was worth four points. Reviewers divided the clips between them. Scores are exploratory, with one review per clip. FFprobe separately checked video dimensions, frame count, frame rate, and duration.
